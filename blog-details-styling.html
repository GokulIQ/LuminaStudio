What to Wear for Your Executive Headshot Session: A Stylist's Guide

Marcus Sterling October 12, 2025 6 Min Read

Your executive headshot is often the very first impression potential investors, recruiters, keynote audiences, and board members have of your personal brand. What you choose to wear communicates competence, authority, and approachability before you even speak.

1. Color Theory: Jewel Tones vs. Harsh Neutrals

When working with Profoto high-speed strobes and softbox diffusion, certain colors react remarkably better than others:

  • The Power of Jewel Tones: Deep navy, royal sapphire, forest emerald, rich burgundy, and slate charcoal create beautiful skin tone separation without overpowering your face.
  • Avoid Solid Pure White: Pure white button-downs can bounce excessive strobe light back into the camera sensor, resulting in clipped highlights. Pair white shirts with a tailored blazer in navy or slate.
  • Avoid Solid Jet Black without Texture: Black absorbs light and can turn into a flat silhouette unless the fabric has defined weave, herringbone, or lapel structure.
Stylist Pro-Tip: The 3-Outfit Rule

Always bring three distinct tops to your studio session: 1 formal board-ready suit/blazer, 1 smart-casual knit or open-collar shirt, and 1 signature piece representing your creative or industry style.

2. Necklines, Collars & Tailoring

The neckline frames your jawline. Unstructured collars can sag under studio gravity:

For Men: Crisp dress collars with collar stays are essential. If shooting without a tie, ensure the second button stance doesn't plunge too low.

For Women: V-neck, boatneck, and tailored blazer lapels elongate the neck and create strong symmetry in LinkedIn circle thumbnails.

3. Eyeglasses & Glare Prevention

If you wear glasses daily, you should wear them in your headshot. However, non-reflective AR (Anti-Reflective) coated lenses are critical. Our studio uses specialized 45° cross-polarizing catchlights to eliminate glare without asking you to remove lenses.
