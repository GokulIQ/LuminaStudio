HM Passport Office Digital Code

35x45mm light grey background with instant gov.uk online verification code.

Case Study

Flawless HMPO Compliance

For this session, the client required an instant digital photo code for the UK HM Passport Office online renewal system. Strict ICAO 9303 standards dictate that there must be absolutely no background shadows and the face must be evenly lit.

We utilized a dual-softbox setup with a dedicated background strobe to achieve the precise light grey background required by the UK government. The resulting image passed the automated gov.uk checker on the first attempt, saving the client time and hassle.

Project Specifications
  • Format: UK Digital Photo Code & 35x45mm Prints
  • Lighting: Flat even lighting, zero shadow
  • Background: Certified Light Grey
  • Processing Time: 5 minutes (Instant Delivery)
