Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about passport compliance, studio appointments, or print formats?

We offer a 100% Government Acceptance Guarantee. If your photo is rejected for any biometric or technical reason, we will retake and reprint your photo completely free of charge, or provide a 100% full refund.

For US passport and visa photos, eyeglasses are strictly prohibited by the US Department of State. For other countries (UK, Schengen, Canada), we strongly advise removing glasses to eliminate flash reflections on the cornea.

Walk-ins are always welcome for all Passport and ID photo services! For executive headshots, creative studio portraits, and family sessions, we recommend booking online to reserve our studio lighting setup and master photographer.

Passport & ID photos are ready within 15 minutes while you wait. Executive headshots and studio portraits are uploaded to your private online gallery within 24 hours with full high-resolution downloads.
