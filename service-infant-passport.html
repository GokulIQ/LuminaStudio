Infant & Newborn Passport Photos

Getting passport photos for babies under 12 months requires patience, gentle handling, and specialized equipment. Our white cradle lighting setup keeps infants calm while meeting strict embassy biometric standards.

View Requirements

Why Parents Trust LuminaStudio for Baby Passport Photos

Standard passport booths cannot accommodate infants. Embassy guidelines require eyes open (or as open as possible for newborns), no pacifiers, plain white background, and precise head positioning � all while keeping your baby comfortable and safe.

White Cradle Lighting Pod Heated, padded cradle with overhead diffused lighting eliminates shadows on tiny faces.
Patient, Certified Baby Photographers Our team is trained in newborn handling and works at your baby's pace � no rushing.
100% Embassy Acceptance Guarantee Compliant with US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Schengen infant passport photo rules.
