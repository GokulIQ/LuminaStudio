Passport & Biometric Photo Questions

Every biometric passport and visa photograph taken at LuminaStudio is audited through our multi-point biometric AI scanner against official embassy rules (ICAO 9303, US State Dept, UK HMPO, Schengen). In the extremely rare event an official authority rejects your photo, we provide an immediate 100% complimentary retake and a full refund.

For US passport and visa photos, prescription eyeglasses have been strictly prohibited since 2016. For UK, Schengen, and Canadian applications, glasses are permitted only if they do not cause reflections or cover the eyes. We recommend taking photos without glasses or jewelry to ensure zero risk of embassy rejection.

Yes! For British passport renewals, we generate an official HMPO photo code. You simply paste this 9-digit code into your online gov.uk passport renewal form, and your photo is instantly fetched into their system.

Our walk-in and booked passport sessions take approximately 10 to 15 minutes. Your high-resolution biometric prints (set of 4 or 6) and digital files are handed to you or emailed within 15 to 30 minutes total.
