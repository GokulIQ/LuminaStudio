Case Study

Dynamic Editorial Leadership

This portfolio session was commissioned for a Forbes feature profiling a leading Silicon Valley fintech founder. The goal was to move away from the traditional, stiff corporate headshot and capture a portrait that exuded innovation, warmth, and modern leadership.

We utilized a striking dual-strobe rim lighting setup to separate the subject from the warm, vibrant background. The high-contrast grading and natural posing resulted in an award-winning editorial image perfect for Tier-1 media publication.

Project Specifications
  • Client: Fintech Founder / CEO
  • Lighting: High-Contrast Rim Light
  • Usage: Forbes Magazine & PR Media Kits
  • Retouching: Magazine-Grade Frequency Separation
